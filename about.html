About the Project

What began as an ordinary campus assignment - a solar system in Augmented Reality - grew into something far larger.

Origin

Astranium started as a group project for a university course: an AR-based solar system explorer for Android, built with Unity, AR Foundation, and ARCore. The original team scoped a functional taster of the solar system - 11 planets, 13 constellations, marker-based tracking.

One team member, however, saw potential beyond the assignment brief. Taking the initiative to develop a parallel Desktop Version, they expanded the project into a full cosmic narrative - the Five Ages of the Universe.

Technical Overview

The project spans two branches that coexist in the same Unity project, sharing assets, scripts, and design language while targeting different platforms.

Branch

AR Version

4 scenes - MainMenu, ScanningMarker, Angkasa, LibraryPlanet. Marker-based solar system with 11 planets and 13 constellations. Fully intact and build-ready.

Branch

Desktop Version

8 scenes - MainMenuDesktop, AngkasaDesktop, LibraryPlanetDesktop, and the Five Ages series. WASD + Mouse Look, orbit mechanics, and custom VFX.

Project Status

Active Development

Primordial, Stelliferous, and Degenerate eras are playable. Black Hole and Dark eras are in planning. A loading screen system for seamless scene transitions is on the roadmap.

Challenges

Developing a project of this scope alone comes with its share of setbacks. A YAML corruption incident during the development of AngkasaDesktop - caused by editing the scene file externally while Unity was open - taught a hard lesson in workflow discipline. Scene recovery was performed from a backup.

The entire project is built by a solo developer, assisted by Ocha, an AI CLI assistant that aids in scripting, YAML analysis, Unity log debugging, documentation, and cosmic narrative crafting.

An independent interactive project exploring the life cycle of the universe through real-time 3D experiences built with Unity.
